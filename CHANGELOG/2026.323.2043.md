# 2026.323.2043 — docs(check): eventually is the poll, timeout is the cap — and how to read summary.yml's two denominators

## Summary

Two hard-won facts about authoring `plan:` steps, both from the Strata family's GPU bed during the pod
extraction (`opencharly/opencharly#1174`), where they cost a red bed and a blocked PR body in turn.

Head: `4a71a5f` · 1 file, +17

**1. `timeout:` is a hard CAP; `eventually:` is the RETRY MATCHER.** The skill already said `timeout:`
is mandatory for a long step, which is true and not enough: a runtime probe into a service with any
start-up cost is a **poll by nature**. Measured — a single-shot `/health` probe failed with
`connection reset` against a server whose cold start legitimately takes minutes, and the CHECK was the
defect, not the server: on the same bed, the projection needed **20 attempts over 4m55s** on a cold
volume and **5 over 1m1.7s** on a warm one. `eventually: "1800s"` + `retry_interval: "15s"` makes it
poll, and the cap must sit ABOVE the `eventually:` window so it never cuts the poll short. The reason
this matters beyond one bed: **a check that fails for being EARLY teaches operators to ignore the
gate**, which is worse than no check.

**2. `summary.yml` carries two denominators that must not be added.** The bed's heading
(`PASS (steps=16, skipped_checks=12)`) counts **phases** and **declared CHECK ids that did not run**
across the whole run; a phase's own report (`45 steps: 35 passed, 0 failed, 10 skipped`) counts **that
phase's steps** and **skipped STEPS**. On this bed, `skipped_checks=12` is 8 runtime-context checks
skipped in `check-image` (box mode has no runtime context) plus a composed candy's `agent-check` in
each of four live phases, while `check-live` alone reports 45 steps of which 10 skipped — the numbers
are unrelated. Pasting the two as one witness is exactly what got a PR body blocked; the rule is to
name the scope of every line in a paste.
