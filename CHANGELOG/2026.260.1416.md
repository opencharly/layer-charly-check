# 2026.260.1416 — feat(skill): add the jetkvm skill entity

## Summary of changes

Adds `jetkvm-skill`, the marketplace skill for the `jetkvm:` check/control verb served out-of-process by `candy/plugin-jetkvm`. It is the analogue of the existing `vnc-skill` and `adb-skill` siblings, owned by this family's concept candy (`charly-check`).

- `charly.yml`: two related edits — the new sibling `skill:` entity (+142 lines), and **one added trigger row in the existing `check-skill` entity** registering the `jetkvm:` verb alongside the family's other out-of-process probe verbs (`adb`, `appium`, `cdp`, `vnc`, …). The check-skill trigger list is the R0 dispatcher source for the check family, so a verb without a row there would not match on a JetKVM/IP-KVM trigger.

The content documents: the authoring shape (scalar method vs the `jetkvm:` map with its exclusive fields, `context: [runtime]` only); the method catalog split into read-only / mutating / never-autonomous; the device-safety gate (`allow_control: true` for every mutating method, and `factory-reset`/`update` refused even then); credential resolution via `verb:credential` (`password_secret:`) or the `JETKVM_AUTH_TOKEN` / `JETKVM_PASSWORD` env vars; the `host` / `insecure` connection contract; and the measured video caveat.

**Verified against the plugin's released mainline:** `opencharly/plugin-jetkvm` at `2892333a64b442f3e952aad7f8639d6790e172f5`, tag **`v2026.260.1344`**. The safety contract (`rpc` is MUTATING and gated; the escape hatch refuses the irreversible reimaging methods) merged in `opencharly/plugin-jetkvm#3` (tag `v2026.260.1317`); the catalog was corrected and pinned in `#4` (tag `v2026.260.1344`).

**Closes / relates to:** complements `opencharly/plugin-jetkvm`.
