### Why this leg ran as ordered units

`/charly-internals:cutover-policy` (the `Detail:` owner of `AGENTS.md`'s HC section) governs ONE contract
"too large to land in a single atomic commit" by splitting it "into ordered units as FORWARD MOTION only".
The single atomic landing was attempted FIRST and is open as `opencharly/layer-charly-check#46`: fourteen
blocks removed, none added, nineteen `SKILL.md` files, 5783 insertions — and its **one** verdict is not a
finding, it is the review engine reporting `finish_reason="length"` over that diff, "NOT retryable with the
same max_tokens/reasoning_effort". The units were ordered, each was reviewed on its own diff, and none
deferred anything at its own merge; the last one, this PR, closes the tracking issue. The missing pointer
from `AGENTS.md`'s HC summary to that axis — the reason this leg's first unit drew an HC BLOCK — is filed as
`opencharly/opencharly#544`.

### Why beside the owning candy

`sdk/deploykit/skills_collect.go` resolves a body as `filepath.Join(layer.GetSourceDir(), ".agents/skills")`
— the directory of the candy that declares the node — and `candywalk.ReadSkillFile` reads
`<that tree>/<name>/SKILL.md`. In this repository the declaring candy is `charly-check`, whose manifest *is*
the repo-root `charly.yml`, so `.agents/skills/` at the repo root **is** the tree beside the owning candy.
That is also why the negative control moves a body *out of* that tree rather than "to the repo root": here
those two paths are the same place.
