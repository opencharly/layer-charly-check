## Summary

**Adds two sections to the `check` skill entity**, in this repo — the source of the `check` family's projections.

1. **The three-step bed shape** — assert the **absence** before the change, perform the change, assert the **presence** afterwards in terms only the change could produce. *A bed that only inspects the final state cannot tell "my change worked" from "something else made it true", and cannot fail without the change.* **Six PRs in one leg failed by omitting step 1.** `spec/245`'s `check-spec-delivery` is the working example named in the text.
2. **The uppercase-token trap** — a step's body is scanned for variables **including its comments**, and an unresolvable upper-case token makes the step **SKIP rather than fail**, so a skipped step reads as a satisfied one. **The line to check after a run is `2 steps: 2 passed, 0 skipped`.**
